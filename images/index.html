Not connecting? Pair manually (no server)

1) Share your screen above. 2) Create the pairing text and send it to your iPhone (Universal Clipboard, AirDrop or Messages). 3) Paste the iPhone's reply here and tap Connect.

Tips for readable text: share a virtual second display set to a LOW resolution such as 1280×720 (apps like DeskPad or BetterDisplay can create one), so everything is drawn big, and keep this tab open. In Chrome you can crank the capture to 60fps; Safari works too.

iPhone: headset viewer

Enter the code from your Mac, or leave it blank to test camera passthrough on its own. Then tap Start, rotate to landscape and drop it in the headset.

Not connecting? Pair manually (no server)
